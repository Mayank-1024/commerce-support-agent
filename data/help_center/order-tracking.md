# Order tracking

## Finding your order status
You can check an order's status with your order number (it starts with ORD-) and the email address used at checkout. Statuses are processing, shipped, delivered, and cancelled.

## Tracking numbers
A tracking number is emailed when your order ships. Carrier tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Late or missing packages
If tracking shows delivered but you cannot find the package, check with neighbors and your building's mail room, then contact us within 7 days so we can open a carrier claim.
