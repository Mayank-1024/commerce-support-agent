# Payment

## Accepted payment methods
We accept major credit and debit cards, digital wallets, and store gift cards. We do not accept checks or cash on delivery.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorized when you place an order and charged when the order ships. If an order is cancelled before shipping, the authorization is released by your bank, usually within 3 to 5 business days.

## Billing disputes
For a charge you do not recognize or a billing dispute, ask for a support specialist. Agents in chat cannot view or change payment details.
