# Returns policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The window is counted in calendar days from the day the carrier marked the package delivered. Items must be unworn or unused, with tags attached.

## Final sale items
Items marked final sale cannot be returned or exchanged. This includes sample sale and clearance items, candles, and gift cards. The final sale label appears on the product page and on your order confirmation.

## Orders that have not arrived
A return can only be started after an order has been delivered. If your order is still processing or in transit, check its status first; a processing order can sometimes be cancelled instead.

## How refunds work
Once a return is approved we email a prepaid return label. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the item. Original shipping charges are not refunded.
