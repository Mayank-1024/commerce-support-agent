# Shipping

## Shipping options and times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days within the contiguous United States. Expedited shipping takes 2 business days. Orders placed before 1 pm Eastern on a business day leave our warehouse the same day.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders of $75 or more. Below $75, standard shipping is a flat $6.95. Expedited shipping is $18 regardless of order size.

## Where we ship
We currently ship to all 50 US states and to APO/FPO addresses. We do not ship internationally yet.
