# Warranty

## What is covered
Outerwear, footwear and outdoor gear carry a one-year warranty against manufacturing defects such as failed seams, zippers, or delaminating soles. Normal wear and tear and accidental damage are not covered.

## Making a warranty claim
Warranty claims are handled by a support specialist, who will ask for your order number and photos of the defect. Approved claims are repaired or replaced.
